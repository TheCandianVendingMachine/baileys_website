What is Tailscale?

Tailscale is a company that produces a VPN. This VPN allows a connection between two clients, peer-to-peer, without needing any changes to existing network architectures. This lets it be fairly plug-and-play so that customers don't need to fiddle with ports or IPs. Likewise, they provide an identity-based access-control-list so you don't need to assign static IPs and ports and whatnot.

The main benefit of Tailscale is that it is encrypted end-to-end, and that your connection will always go through. They achieve the latter via the ICE protocol. If you can't connect via IPv6, IPv4, or the NAT traversals, they fallback to HTTPS relay servers that stream your requests through Tailscale operated servers.
